MILL ON MOBILE

A clear next step.

Use the same Mill account in the app and at app.mill.chat. Your workspace access is managed by your company’s owner or administrator.

I’m not receiving notifications

Allow notifications for Mill in your phone’s Settings. Then check notification preferences in Mill and make sure notifications are enabled for the websites you manage. Quiet hours and your notification preferences can affect delivery.

I can’t see a company or conversation

Check that you’re signed in with the invited email address. Ask the company owner to confirm your membership and role. If the issue continues, contact support with the company name and the steps you took.

A message or attachment failed to send

Check your connection and the send status before retrying. If the issue continues, send us the app version, device model, time of the problem and a screenshot with private information hidden. Never send passwords or verification codes.

How do I manage billing?

Workspace owners and members with billing access can manage subscriptions in the web portal. Contact support if you need help locating the right workspace or understanding a charge.

Privacy & account requests

To delete your login account, use Account settings in Mill or open account deletion in the web portal. Deleting a company is a separate action and keeps your login account. For access, correction or other privacy requests, email us from your account address. We may verify your identity and explain how shared company data is affected.
