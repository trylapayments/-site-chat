All questions & answers ↗
Can I try Mill without a credit card?

Yes. Start a 14-day free trial without entering payment details. Explore your workspace, customise the widget and see how Mill fits your team.

Can each website have a different widget?

Yes. Each website can have its own branding, welcome messages, availability and rules. Your operators can manage the conversations together in one workspace.

Will AI replace our operators?

You choose how AI helps. Use Mill AI for suggestions your operators review, or let the AI agent answer routine questions and pass the conversation to a person when needed.

What happens when we use our included AI allowance?

Your human-powered live chat keeps working. Add more AI credits through Billing when you need extra AI capacity.

Are plans priced per operator?

Each plan includes a bundle of operator seats. Starter includes 3, Essential 5, Growth 10 and Business 20. Choose monthly billing or pay yearly for two months free.
