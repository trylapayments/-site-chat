Do I need to be a developer?

You need access to your website’s custom-code area, or someone who can add a small installation script for you. Mill gives you the code to copy.

Can visitors leave a message when we are away?

Yes. You can let visitors leave a message, or hide the widget when your team is offline or outside opening hours.

Can I control where my widget is installed?

Yes. Add your website to Allowed domains in the installation settings.

Can my team use their own names and photos?

Yes. Agents can add their name and profile photo in My profile.

Can customers rate a conversation?

Yes. Conversation ratings are optional and can be enabled in your workspace settings.

Can I hide read receipts?

Yes. Whether customers see that their message has been read is controlled in settings. Customer read receipts are off by default.

Can AI answer visitors and help my team?

Yes. The AI agent answers visitors using your business knowledge. Mill AI suggests replies and summaries for your operators, who review the final answer before sending.

How do I manage or change my subscription?

Workspace owners and people with billing access can manage their plan and payment methods in Mill. See Pricing for the billing rules.
