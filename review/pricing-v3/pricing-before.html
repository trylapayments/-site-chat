More questions & answers ↗
Can I try Mill without a credit card?

Yes. Start a 14-day free trial without adding a payment method. Mill has paid plans, with time to explore before you choose.

Are these prices per operator?

No. Each price is for your workspace and includes the operator seats shown above.

How does annual billing work?

Pay for ten months and get a full year. Annual totals are $290 for Starter, $490 for Essential, $890 for Growth and $1,990 for Business. Annual subscriptions are paid upfront and renew yearly.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately, with a prorated adjustment shown before you confirm. Downgrades take effect at your next renewal.

When does my billing month start?

Your billing period starts when your paid subscription begins. It renews on that date, rather than the first of the calendar month.

How much AI is included?

Essential includes 100 AI conversations per month, Growth includes 500 and Business includes 1,000. Starter has no included AI. Buy additional AI credits in your Mill workspace when you need them.
