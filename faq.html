13 questions

Do I need to be a developer?

You need access to your website’s custom-code area, or someone who can add a small installation script for you. Mill gives you the code to copy.

Can visitors leave a message when we are away?

Yes. You can let visitors leave a message, or hide the widget when your team is offline or outside opening hours.

Can I control where my widget is installed?

Yes. Add your website to Allowed domains in the installation settings.

Can my team use their own names and photos?

Yes. Agents can add their name and profile photo in My profile.

Can customers rate a conversation?

Yes. Conversation ratings are optional and can be enabled in your workspace settings.

Can I hide read receipts?

Yes. Whether customers see that their message has been read is controlled in settings. Customer read receipts are off by default.

Can AI answer visitors and help my team?

Yes. The AI agent answers visitors using your business knowledge. Mill AI suggests replies and summaries for your operators, who review the final answer before sending.

How do I manage or change my subscription?

Workspace owners and people with billing access can change plans and payment methods inside Mill. An upgrade starts immediately, with the prorated charge shown before you confirm. A downgrade takes effect at your next billing renewal.

Can I try Mill without a credit card?

Yes. Start a 14-day free trial without adding a payment method. Mill has paid plans, with time to explore before you choose.

Are these prices per operator?

No. Each price is for your workspace and includes the operator seats listed on the Pricing page.

How does annual billing work?

Pay for ten months and get a full year. Annual totals are $290 for Starter, $490 for Essential, $890 for Growth and $1,990 for Business. Annual subscriptions are paid upfront and renew yearly.

When does my billing month start?

Your billing period starts when your paid subscription begins. It renews on that date, rather than the first of the calendar month.

How much AI is included?

Essential includes 100 AI conversations per month, Growth includes 500 and Business includes 1,000. Starter has no included AI. Buy additional AI credits in your Mill workspace when you need them.
